How access works

Principle: everything needed to decide, register, and understand the event stays public. Anything that provides extra training, deep know-how, and detailed submissions is shared here for members/participants.

  • Public: objectives, theme & topics, timeline, deliverables overview, judging criteria (high-level), benefits, prizes, venue/Zoom, CPD points, contacts, public social links.
  • Members/participants: training materials, past developed use cases, templates, recordings, deep-dive workflows, and internal sharing.

✋ You're signed in, but not registered yet

To access members-only content, you need to register as a participant first.

How to get access:

  1. Register via the form: Open Registration Form
  2. Use the same email: Register with the email you're currently signed in with ()
  3. Wait for approval: Your email will be added to the members list (usually within 24 hours)
  4. Come back: Sign in again and you'll see all the members-only content below

What you'll get access to:

  • 🏆 Winners Library: Explore past winning projects from 2024 and 2025
  • 📚 AI/AECO Resources: Comprehensive resource hub with tools and frameworks
  • 📅 AI Sharing Sessions: Venue, Zoom links, and full schedule
  • 🎓 Training Materials: Templates, recordings, and deep-dive workflows

Already registered via the form? Contact the organizers at youth@sia.org.sg or yal.aiseries@gmail.com to check your registration status.
